# 🌳 A Student’s Guide to the AI Universe: AI, ML, DL & Generative AI Explained

*Breaking down Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Generative AI in simple words with relatable real-world examples.*

---

## ✨ Introduction

Artificial Intelligence has become part of our everyday lives. From voice assistants and recommendation systems to image generation and smart search, we hear terms like AI, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Generative AI more than ever.

When I first started learning them, they felt confusing because they were often mentioned together.

It was hard to understand what each one meant and how they were connected.

The comparison that helped me most was thinking of them like a growing tree.

Artificial Intelligence is the trunk.

Machine Learning grows as a major branch.

Deep Learning grows further from Machine Learning.

And Generative AI becomes one of the newest creative branches.

Once I understood that connection, the topic started feeling much easier.

This repository shares that learning in a simple beginner-friendly way.

---
